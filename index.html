Who I am

My name is Jordan Barden. I am a graduate of the University of Michigan school of engineering. I studied computer science and focused on interactive media. Outside of my classes I participated in the student-run wolverinesoft game development studio as a technical artist. I have practiced 3D modeling for about 9 years beginning with product design (Solidworks) in high school before transitioning to game art (Blender). I have helped ship 3 games to steam and built my own custom game engine as well.

Skills

My programming skills include C#, C++, C, Lua, Python, Html, matlab. I have the C/C++ libraries SDL, GLM, Lua + Luabridge, Rapidjson, and Box2D. For communication and logistics, I have learned jira, bitbucket, confluence, git/github desktop, discord, and the agile workflow. As for 3D art, I have learned basic skills for modeling, sculpting, retopology, rigging, weight painting, UV unwrapping, some basic texturing, and some basic animation.

Projects and Experience

Wolverinesoft Studio

A student run gamedev studio I participated in as a 3D/ technical artist and lead artist. Over a year, I helped ship 3 games to steam. I set up 2D and 3D animation controllers, set up particle effects/vfx, made UI, facilitated cross discipline communication, rigged/animated 2D assets, and modeled/rigged 3D assets. The games I helped ship: Cellosium, Tails of War, and Soul of the Forest

2D Multiplatform Game Engine

I made a custom cross platform game engine. It can build for windows, mac, and linux. It uses SDL for graphics, Box2D for physics, and Lua/LuaBridge for scripting. GLM and Rapidjson were also used for other functionality.

Videogame Development Class Projects

Over a 6 week period, 4 others and I built a small puzzle exploration game. We followed the agile workflow and did playtests every 1 to 2 weeks for iteration feedback. I worked on the movement, flashlight, and light effect mechanics.

Personal Projects

I frequently study stylized 3D models and I am currently in the early stages of a new unannounced game prototype.
