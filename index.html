Who I am

I graduated from the University of Michigan's School of Engineering in May 2025. My degree is in Computer Science, and I focused my classes in interactive media like videogames and websites. Alongside classes for coding theory and optimization, I took classes for videogame development, software development, videogame engine architecture, introduction to AI, and Web Systems.

Outside of my classes, I participated in UofM's videogame studio wolverinesoft and the UofM's animation club. In wolverinesoft, I had worked as an artist, technical artists, and Lead Artist; furthermore, I have help ship 4 games from the studio to Steam and Itch.io. See My Projects for more details.

Additionally, for nearly 9 years I have studied various 3D modeling programs and skills. In my freshmen year of highschool I started learning the CAD software Solidworks and actively worked with it through my senior year. In my junior year of highschool, I started learning 3D modeling in Blender. Sense then, I have learned skills related to modeling, sculpting, manual retopology, rigging, weightpainting, UV unwrapping, basic texturing, animation, and exporting to Unity.
