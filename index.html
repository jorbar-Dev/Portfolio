Who I am

My name is Jordan Barden. I am a graduate of the University of Michigan school of engineering. I studied computer science and focused on interactive media. Outside of my classes I participated in the student-run wolverinesoft game development studio as a technical artist. I have practiced 3D modeling for about 9 years beginning with product design (Solidworks) in high school before transitioning to game art (Blender). I have helped ship 3 games to steam and built my own custom game engine as well.

Skills

My programming skills include C#, C++, C, Lua, Python, Html, matlab. I have the C/C++ libraries SDL, GLM, Lua + Luabridge, Rapidjson, and Box2D. For communication and logistics, I have learned jira, bitbucket, confluence, git/github desktop, discord, and the agile workflow. As for 3D art, I have learned basic skills for modeling, sculpting, retopology, rigging, weight painting, UV unwrapping, some basic texturing, and some basic animation.

Projects and Experience

2D Multiplatform Game Engine

I made a custom cross platform game engine through a game engine architecture course. It can build for windows, mac, and linux. It uses SDL for graphics, Box2D for physics, and Lua/LuaBridge for scripting. GLM and Rapidjson were also used for other functionality.
