Who I am

I graduated from the University of Michigan's School of Engineering in May 2025. My degree is in Computer Science, and I focused my classes in interactive media like videogames and websites. Alongside classes for coding theory and optimization, I took classes for videogame development, software development, videogame engine architecture, introduction to AI, and Web Systems.

Outside of my classes, I participated in UofM's videogame studio wolverinesoft and the UofM's animation club. In wolverinesoft, I had worked as an artist, technical artists, and Lead Artist; furthermore, I have help ship 4 games from the studio to Steam and Itch.io. See My Projects for more details.

Additionally, for nearly 9 years I have studied various 3D modeling programs and skills. In my freshmen year of highschool I started learning the CAD software Solidworks and actively worked with it through my senior year. In my junior year of highschool, I started learning 3D modeling in Blender. Sense then, I have learned skills related to modeling, sculpting, manual retopology, rigging, weightpainting, UV unwrapping, basic texturing, animation, and exporting to Unity.

Wolverinesoft Studio

Wolverinesoft Studio is a student ran game dev studio from the University of Michigan. I have worked alongside teams of 30 to 50 people over 3 cycles. All games were made with Unity and Git, and communication was over Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, and Discord.

Cellosium

For Cellosium I was both an artist and tech artist. Alongside some 3D art and concept art, I made the directional eye and flinch mechanics for enemies. I created the 2D art asset, imported and set up its animation states, and created the script that controlled which animation to play.

Tails of War

For Tails of War I was a lead artist. I was tasked to work with the art lead to redesign the art style. With this I helped with 3D art hiring, and finding a style that the team could complete. During the cycle, I modeled, rigged, and weight painted character armors. I helped implement the art assets into the game. I also communicated between different members of leadership, animators, and modelers to make sure the project's needs were meant.

Soul of the forest

For this project I worked as a tech artist. Most of the semester I was animating and implementing 2D rigged sprites for the battle system. I also created the system that spawned the battle damage effects and implemented them as well. I helped work on the quest announcement UI; I made the system that handled the text appearing and scaling on screen.

Corrupt Eldritch Order

I briefly came onto this project while working on Soul of the Forest to help rig and animate the 2D player characters. I also implemented the animated sprites.
